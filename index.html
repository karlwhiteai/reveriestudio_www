Where the sea glows

A woman in a dark wool coat stands ankle-deep in the surf on a black-sand beach at blue hour, in the right third of the frame, and every wave that breaks around her feet glows vibrant and bright HDR electric blue. Photoreal cinematic footage. Tiny motes of the same vibrant HDR blue light lift off the water and rise slowly into the air around her like rain falling upward. She slowly raises one open hand and a few motes drift toward it and hover over her palm, lighting her face softly from below. Tall sea stacks stand in the mist behind her, and the left side is calm dark sea and soft mist. The camera dollies in slowly at waist height. Deep blue dusk, cold mist, natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, film grain. Gentle surf and a low, airy hum.

Features

Everything in one studio.

Eight tools that start from words or a recording, and one library that keeps all of it.

Text to Speech

Turn writing into natural speech, with built-in voices.

Your own voice

Clone a voice from a short recording, or train one from your own clips.

Speech to Text

Transcribe audio and video, with subtitles ready to export.

Text to Music

Write a style and lyrics, and get a full song, sung or instrumental.

Text to Image

Describe a picture and watch it appear.

Edit Image

Change a picture by saying what to change.

Text to Video

Bring a scene to life as a short, moving shot.

Library and Projects

Everything you make, in one place. Search it, star it, gather it into projects.

On the way: stem separation, noise reduction, video upscaling, Apple's own voices, and cover art for every song.

The app

Built for the Mac you have.

One window for everything you make. Each tool opens on what you are about to create.

Your Mac

Sized to your Mac.

Reverie Studio checks your Mac's memory and offers the models that fit it. Bigger Macs get bigger models; every Mac gets every kind of media.

16 GBand up
  • Speech with built-in voices, and quick voice clones
  • Transcription and subtitles
  • Full songs, images, image edits and short video

Measured on a Mac mini with M4: an image in about three minutes.

36–48 GB
  • Everything above, with larger, sharper models
  • Train your own voice from 32 GB
  • Tuned voices from 24 GB

Larger image and edit models that follow your words more closely.

64 GBand up
  • The largest image, video and music models
  • Room for a song and a video in the same evening
  • Several tools working at once

Measured on a Mac Studio with M3 Ultra: an image in about 30 seconds.

Private

No account. No tracking. Works entirely offline once your models are in place.

  • Your prompts and lyrics
  • Your recordings and voices
  • Every song, image and video
  • Your projects and favorites

Goes online

  • Model downloads, from their publishers, when you choose them
  • Nothing else
Connections

The studio for your whole home.

Reverie Studio keeps working in the background, so your smart home, your chats, your scripts and your phone can all make things with it. Everything still happens on your Mac.

Home AssistantVoices and listening for your smart home

Give your home a voice. Even yours.

Announcements, reminders and Assist replies in any Reverie Studio voice, including one you trained on your own recordings. It listens as well: speech to text over Wyoming, the protocol Home Assistant already speaks. No cloud voice service, no subscription, nothing leaves the house.
