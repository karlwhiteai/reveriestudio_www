Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026. Last updated November 17, 2026.

In short

  • Reverie Studio makes speech, music, images and video on your Mac. Your prompts, recordings, voices and everything you make stay there.
  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting in the app. We cannot see what you make.
  • The app goes online only to download the models you choose, and when you buy or restore Pro through the Mac App Store.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no trackers. If you email us, we keep your message to reply.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Who we are

Reverie Studio and this website, reveriestudio.app, are made by Better Than Expected, LLC, 195 41st St, PO Box 11095, Oakland, CA 94611-9991, United States (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the information described here. Write to us at hello@reveriestudio.app about anything in this policy.

This policy covers the Reverie Studio app for Mac and this website. It does not cover other companies’ services that you choose to use with the app, such as Hugging Face, Home Assistant or Open WebUI; their own policies apply to them.

The app: what stays on your Mac

Reverie Studio does its work on your Mac. Everything you put in and everything it makes is stored on your Mac, in your user account:

  • What you make: songs, images, video, speech and transcripts, in your Documents folder under Reverie.
  • What you type and record: prompts, lyrics, text to speak, recordings and uploaded files, together with the settings used for each result.
  • Your voices: voice samples, voice training recordings and the voices trained from them.
  • Your library: projects, favorites, saved prompts and notes.
  • App data: settings, job history, logs and downloaded models, in your Library folder under Application Support.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it, and the app has no account to link it to. You can delete any item in the app, and removing the app’s folders removes everything. Because your work exists only on your Mac, keep your own backups; we cannot recover it for you.

Some pictures and videos carry a small visible “Reverie Studio” mark. The mark is added on your Mac and sends nothing anywhere.

The app: when it goes online

Model downloads. When you choose to download a model, the app fetches it from Hugging Face, where we and the model publishers host the files. Like any web server, Hugging Face receives your IP address and the files requested, under its own privacy policy. Nothing you have made is sent. Once your models are in place, the app works entirely offline.

Models that need your own account. A few optional models are published under licences their owners ask you to accept on Hugging Face. For those, you sign in to Hugging Face and give the app an access token. The app uses the token for that download only; it does not save it to disk or write it to a log, and it never sends it to us.

Purchases. Reverie Studio Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy and shares with us only what it shares with every developer, such as sales totals by country; we do not receive your name or payment details. Your purchase is checked on your Mac through Apple. If we ever sell Reverie Studio another way, we will update this policy first.

Nothing else. The app does not check in with us, report usage, send crash reports or show advertising.

The app: connections you set up

You can let other software and devices use Reverie Studio, for example Home Assistant for voices and listening, Open WebUI for media from your chats, your own scripts through the app’s local interface, or your phone. These connections run between your own devices. Requests and results travel directly between them, not through us, and the other software’s own terms and policies apply to what it does. Turn a connection on only for devices and networks you trust.

When the app uses a language model to refine your prompts, it uses one on your Mac: Apple Intelligence’s on-device model or one you run yourself. Reverie Studio does not use Apple’s Private Cloud Compute or any other online model.

This website

reveriestudio.app is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no analytics or advertising, and loads nothing from any other website, not even fonts.

Like every website, it has to be delivered by servers. The pages are hosted by GitHub and delivered through Cloudflare’s network. When you visit, they receive the technical details any browser sends, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and keep them briefly in logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. They act under their own privacy policies. We do not receive those logs; Cloudflare may show us totals, such as the number of visits, that do not identify anyone.

When you email us

If you write to us, for example to be told when Reverie Studio is available, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply, to tell you about the launch if you asked, and to support you. Our email provider stores messages for us and may not use them for anything else. If you asked to hear about the launch, we keep your address until we have told you and then delete it. Other messages we keep for as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than two years after our last exchange, unless the law requires otherwise. Ask us at any time and we will delete them and stop writing to you.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information, including for targeted advertising.
  • We do not use your prompts, recordings, voices or creations to train models. We never receive them.
  • We do not build profiles of you or combine information about you from other sources.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, ask for a copy, and object to how we use it. In practice we hold very little: only what you send us by email. Write to hello@reveriestudio.app; we will answer within a month, or sooner where the law requires, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on our legitimate interest in answering you and supporting the product for email, and on your consent for launch news, which you can withdraw at any time. You may complain to your local data protection authority. We are based in the United States, so information you send us is handled there.

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have the rights above. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use sensitive personal information to infer anything about you.

Children

Reverie Studio and this website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

Your work is as safe as your Mac: use a login password and FileVault, and keep your Mac up to date. Other devices can reach the app only through connections you turn on, each protected by a key or a list of allowed addresses. Information you send us is kept in services protected by strong passwords and two-factor sign-in.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app or this website handles information, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app or on this website.
